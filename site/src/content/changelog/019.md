Open Progress and scroll to Best time of day. The sentence names the hours you focus best, and you can hover any hour or cell for details. It follows the label filter and has its own 30/90/all-time range.

On your first visit of a new week, last week's recap opens once. You can open it any time with Weekly recap at the top of Progress and use the arrows for earlier weeks. Turn the pop-up off under Settings → Automation.
