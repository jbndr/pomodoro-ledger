title: "Add a site blocker extension that blocks chosen sites during focus"
date: 2026-10-04T17:40:23Z
features: ["site-blocker"]
---

Install unpacked: check out feat/site-blocker, open chrome://extensions (or edge:// or brave://), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the extension/ folder. Open the extension's options, add a site such as youtube.com and allow access when asked. Reload Pomodoro Ledger, and Settings → Automation shows 'Extension connected'. Start a focus round and visit the site: you land on the focus page with the time left. Firefox: about:debugging → Load Temporary Add-on → extension/manifest.json.
