title: "Play rain, café or brown noise during focus"
date: 2026-10-04T17:38:25Z
features: ["soundscapes"]
---

Settings → Sound & alerts → Soundscape: pick Rain, Café or Brown and press Preview. Then start a focus round: it fades in over 3 s and fades out on pause, skip or the end of focus. Turn on Also during breaks to keep it playing between rounds.
