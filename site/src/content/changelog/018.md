title: "Add recurring tasks and roll over unfinished tasks"
date: 2026-10-04T10:57:23Z
features: ["recurring-tasks", "rollover"]
---

Type “Water plants every mon thu” in a new task, or open a task’s When menu and pick Repeat. When you finish one, the next copy shows up on its next day.

On a new day, Today asks about yesterday’s unfinished tasks: pick Move to today or Leave. To always move them or never ask, change it in Settings → Automation → New day.
