---

Hover a task and press the arrow-to-top button, or point at it and press ⌥⌘↑ (Ctrl+Alt+↑ on Windows).

⌘-click tasks (Shift-click for a range, ⌘A for all, long press on a phone), then use the bar at the bottom: Label, Date, To top, Finish or Delete.
