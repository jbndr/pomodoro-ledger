Pomodoro Ledger started as a simple idea: a Pomodoro timer where every focus round is written down against a task, so at the end of the week you can see where your time actually went.

Between 3 and 6 October it went through 82 releases. Some highlights:

## Planning that carries over

Tasks are estimated in rounds. Recurring tasks show up in Today on their own, yesterday's leftovers can roll over, and a weekly plan and review closes the loop.

## A calmer timer

The clock hides its secondary controls until you need them. Flow mode counts up instead of down and suggests a break sized to the session. The rounds start over on a new day.

## The ledger

A weekly recap, your best time of day, and a Year in Focus story — all from the same record of focus minutes.

## Working together

Rooms let you focus alongside other people. Scheduled public sessions send a reminder when they start. Everyone can see the room; nobody can see your tasks.
